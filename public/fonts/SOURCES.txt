The Latin fonts do not provide Chinese glyphs. The reading fallback family
order is kept identical to the reference page, so Chinese glyphs are rendered
by the system/browser fallback and may differ between operating systems.

The reference imports only weight 400. Its bold headings and light inline
code declarations are retained; the browser synthesizes/chooses the available
weight just as on the reference site.
